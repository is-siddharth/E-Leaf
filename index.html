The idea

Learning can become useful to someone else.

E-Leaf is built around a simple cycle. It is not a checklist and it is not a race.

01

Learn

Take classes, explore subjects, and learn from people who know something you want to understand.

02

Contribute

Share notes, ideas, questions, examples, and useful explanations as part of learning.

03

Help

When you understand something, help another learner move forward.

04

Teach

Over time, meaningful contribution can lead to the ability to teach. That capability is earned, not selected from a menu.

Two ways to belong

Global learning or your institution.

The same E-Leaf identity can move between the wider community and an institution that uses E-Leaf for its own learning.

Global

Learn with the wider community.

Explore subjects, courses, Trees, questions, and learning experiences from across E-Leaf.

Institutional

Learn inside your institution.

Follow your institution's curriculum, classes, people, and learning activities.

One identity

Keep being the same person.

Your context changes. Your E-Leaf identity and learning history remain yours.
